Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026.

01About this service

Sanchari is a campus transport service operated by Saichandan Mettu, in coordination with the IIT Hyderabad Transport Department. It lets members of the IIT Hyderabad community pay a fixed transport service fee and receive a digital boarding pass for campus shuttle and outstation bus services. This policy explains the limited information we handle in that process.

02What we collect

  • Name and phone number, entered at the time of payment — used only to identify you as the holder of the pass to the bus staff.
  • Route and payment reference for the journey you paid for, so your pass can be issued and verified.
  • Standard web-server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp) retained temporarily by our hosting provider, as with any website. We do not actively analyse these.

03What we do not collect

  • No card number, UPI ID or PIN, or bank details ever reach this site. Payment details are entered only in the secure checkout of our payment gateway, a provider regulated by the Reserve Bank of India.
  • No cookies, analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers are set by this site. It is static HTML/CSS/JS with no third-party scripts other than the payment gateway checkout.

04How we use it

Only to issue and verify your boarding pass, and to respond to support or refund requests. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information for marketing.

05Who we share it with

  • Our payment gateway, to process your payment — its handling of payment data is governed by its own privacy policy, available on its website.
  • The IIT Hyderabad Transport Department, for operating and verifying travel on its services.
  • Where required by law.

06Retention

Pass and payment records are kept only as long as needed for verification, accounting, and dispute resolution, and are then deleted.

07Your requests & contact

To ask what data we hold about you, or to request correction or deletion, email office.transport@iith.ac.in.

See also our Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation Policy.
