01About this service

Sanchari is a digital service operated by Saichandan Mettu, in coordination with the IIT Hyderabad Transport Department. It lets members of the IIT Hyderabad community pay a fixed transport service fee and receive a boarding pass for campus shuttle and outstation (Patancheru, Miyapur) bus services. Sanchari does not operate the buses itself.

02Payment and your pass

  • The fee for each route is fixed by our server. Neither you nor your browser can alter the amount charged.
  • Payment is collected through the secure checkout of our payment gateway, a provider regulated by the Reserve Bank of India. This site never receives your card, UPI, or banking details.
  • A pass becomes valid only after the payment gateway confirms payment to our server.
  • Each pass is valid only for the route and date shown. Carry the pass and valid ID when you board.

03Your responsibilities

  • Carry valid identification and reach the boarding point on time, per the route information shown in the app.
  • Use the pass only for your own travel. Passes must not be resold or transferred.
